# صور الفواكه الجاهزة

The fruit photos in this folder come from the **Fruits-360** dataset by Mihai Oltean (2017-),
https://github.com/fruits-360, licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes made: the white background was made transparent, each photo was cropped to the fruit, padded to a square,
resized to at most 256 px and saved as WebP. These edited photos are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
